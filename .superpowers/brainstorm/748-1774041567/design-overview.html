Key Design Decisions

  • Dark cinematic theme — fits the movie domain, makes colored rivers/lines pop
  • 4 linked panels — Timeline River (main), Sequel Trajectory, Franchise Dominance, Profile Card
  • Franchise rivers — not traditional bar/line charts; each franchise is a flowing band with movie dots embedded
  • Brushing flows downward — brush the timeline → dominance chart + trajectory chart update with animated transitions
  • Narrative annotation bar — curated insights cycle with ◂ ▸, clicking one highlights relevant data across all panels
  • Dynamic filters — genre, decade range slider, minimum entries, metric selector (revenue/rating/budget)
